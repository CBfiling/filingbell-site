Special situations

Form 8-K is the report a public company files with the SEC to report specified events such as material agreements, bankruptcies and changes in control, and to disclose others voluntarily, such as a strategic review. This page also lists tender offers, merger proxies, going-private statements, registrations and delistings, and companies with several related filings within a set window.

Strength (1 to 5) is FilingBell's rule-based ranking of how definitive or significant the reported event is under fixed criteria: for example, a signed merger agreement ranks above a board saying it is exploring options, and purchases by three or more insiders rank above a single purchase. It is not a rating of the company or its stock and not a prediction of price movement.
