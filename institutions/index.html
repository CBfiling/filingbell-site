Institutions

Schedule 13D or 13G is filed by a person or group that acquires beneficial ownership of more than 5% of a class of a company's registered voting shares. Schedule 13G is a shorter form for certain holders, such as investors who certify they do not seek to change or influence control; others file Schedule 13D, which states the filer's purpose. Amended or late filings can show stakes below 5%. Form 13F is a quarterly report of long positions in certain U.S.-listed securities, filed by investment managers with at least $100 million in those securities within 45 days after each quarter ends, so positions can be several months old. This page covers 13F filings from a fixed list of managers FilingBell follows; inclusion is not an endorsement or a suggestion to follow their holdings.

Strength (1 to 5) is FilingBell's rule-based ranking of how definitive or significant the reported event is under fixed criteria: for example, a signed merger agreement ranks above a board saying it is exploring options, and purchases by three or more insiders rank above a single purchase. It is not a rating of the company or its stock and not a prediction of price movement.
