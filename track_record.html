Track Record

Withdrawn for revision · November 2026

The backtest previously shown on this page has been withdrawn. Its figures should not be relied on.

What was wrong

An audit of the backtest found problems that made its results unreliable:

  • Outcome classification. A company was counted as “acquired” if it had ever reported completing an acquisition — including acquisitions it made itself, and ones before the flagged filing. Most companies labeled “acquired” were in fact still independently listed.
  • Survivorship bias. Companies no longer in the SEC's current ticker list — often the very ones that were actually taken over or went bankrupt — were left out.
  • Wrong security. Some companies were priced using a warrant or unit instead of the common stock.
  • No benchmark. Price changes weren't compared to the overall market.

What comes next

A corrected backtest is planned and will be published here with its full methodology when complete. It will:

  • be clearly labeled as hypothetical results;
  • only count an outcome if it happened after the flagged filing, to the company that filed it;
  • include losers, bankruptcies, and delisted companies;
  • use the common stock, measured over fixed time windows, alongside a market benchmark.

The daily digest previously showed “Historically” figures from the same backtest; those have been removed as well. The digest continues to report filings as they happen, with a link to each primary source.
