Insiders

Form 4 is filed with the SEC by a company's officers, directors and owners of more than 10% of a class of its shares, generally within two business days of a transaction in the company's securities. This page lists reported purchases (transaction code P) of at least $500,000, or that total at least $50,000 and either raise the insider's holdings by at least 20% or are the insider's first reported holding of the security, and purchases by three or more insiders within 30 days. Shares and prices are as reported on the Form 4; values and totals are calculated by FilingBell from them.

Strength (1 to 5) is FilingBell's rule-based ranking of how definitive or significant the reported event is under fixed criteria: for example, a signed merger agreement ranks above a board saying it is exploring options, and purchases by three or more insiders rank above a single purchase. It is not a rating of the company or its stock and not a prediction of price movement.
